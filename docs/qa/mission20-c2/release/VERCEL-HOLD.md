## Stale clients, in-flight requests and failure recovery

Old tabs, workers, cached scripts, JWT holders and direct REST clients can outlive deployment promotion. Native direct INSERT must remain denied by the database; never restore unsafe grants to support them. Legacy child-first writers may receive bounded 55P03/40P01; show controlled refresh/retry feedback without erasing user intent. Recheck authorization/deadlines on safe retry. JWT logout or skew protection is not a database write barrier. Do not automatically replay team/account deletion or other destructive actions after ambiguous responses; reconcile read-only and require deliberate renewed intent.

If DB expansion/contract fails: keep the prior deployment and hold, retain committed freeze, capture SQLSTATE/object/ledger/ACL evidence, and fix forward or execute an independently approved recovery. A committed stage with lost client response is reconciled by exact catalog/ledger checks before any retry. If new app fails after DB success: keep admission and affected writers frozen and promote only a build independently qualified against the tightened database. The historical raw-native-INSERT app is not a functional compatibility rollback. If no compatible rollback exists, remain in maintenance; do not reopen the unsafe boundary.
