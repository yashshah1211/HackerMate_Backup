Solving the Team Formation Bottleneck

Omnikon 2026 features a strict team size of 1 to 2 builders. In a national-level hackathon with multiple evaluation stages, finding the single right partner is the most critical driver of participant success and project submission quality.

The Challenge
  • Discord Chaos: Pinned chats and spamming in `#find-teammates` make it impossible to filter builders by exact skills or track interest.
  • High Attrition Rate: Online hackathons spanning weeks suffer from 50%+ team dropout rates due to poor initial pairing and lack of engagement.
The HackerMate Solution
  • Compatibility Engine: Automated matching based on verified skills, tracks, and college details.
  • Built-In Team Workspaces: Dedicated workspace for messaging, resource mapping, and task management.

Proposed Partnership Deliverables

HackerMate Delivers Value We Ask from Omnikon Marketing
Dedicated Matchmaking Portal: Branded space for Omnikon participants. Official Matching Partner: Listing on Unstop page.
GitHub Verification: Pre-vetted profiles showcasing developer stats. Discord & Email Links: Pin links in discord and confirmation mails.
Retention System: Automated 24h cron email reminder alerts. Logo & Co-marketing: Brand placement in flyers and slide decks.
